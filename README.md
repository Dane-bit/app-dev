## About the Series

**The Mentalist** is an American crime, drama, and mystery television series created by **Bruno Heller**. It originally aired on September 23, 2008, and ended on February 18, 2015.

The story follows Patrick Jane, a former fake psychic who uses his exceptional observation skills and ability to read people to help the police solve different criminal cases.

## Main Character: Patrick Jane

**Patrick Jane**, played by **Simon Baker**, is the main character of the series. He is a former psychic who admits that his psychic abilities were only tricks. Instead, he uses his intelligence, observation skills, and knowledge of human behavior to understand people and solve crimes.

### Patrick Jane's Characteristics

- **Intelligent** – He can understand situations quickly.
- **Observant** – He notices small details that other people usually miss.
- **Manipulative** – He knows how to influence people to reveal information.
- **Funny** – He often uses jokes and humor during serious situations.
- **Confident** – He trusts his abilities when solving cases.
- **Mysterious** – He has a painful past that affects his decisions.

## Story Overview

The series focuses on Patrick Jane, who works as a consultant for the **California Bureau of Investigation (CBI)**. He helps a team of agents investigate and solve different murder cases using his exceptional ability to observe people and understand their behavior.

However, his main purpose is to find a serial killer known as **Red John**, who murdered his wife and daughter. This personal mission becomes one of the biggest parts of the story.

As the series continues, Patrick Jane faces different challenges, meets new people, and develops relationships with the agents he works with.
